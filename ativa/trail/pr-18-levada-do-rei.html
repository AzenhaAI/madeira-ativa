PR 18
Levada do Reiopen

Levada do Rei (PR 18) is a moderate 5.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Quebradas to Ribeiro Bonito. It climbs about 447 m and descends about 283 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 3 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
