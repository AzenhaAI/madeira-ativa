PR1
Vereda do Pico Branco e Terra Chãopen

Vereda do Pico Branco e Terra Chã (PR1) is a easy 2.7 km walking trail on Madeira, running from ER120 to Terra Chã. It climbs about 121 m and descends about 242 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
