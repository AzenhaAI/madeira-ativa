PR 2
Vereda do Urzalopen

Vereda do Urzal (PR 2) is a moderate 10.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Curral das Freiras to Lombo do Urzal. It climbs about 950 m and descends about 1189 m along the way. That averages a sustained climb, about 92 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 458 m and 1438 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 4 h 30. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 4.5 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Only one end is served by a bus (81); the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. No car park is mapped within 600 m of either end.

Common questions

How long does PR 2 Vereda do Urzal take?

The official walking time for PR 2 is 4 h 30 over 10.3 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 2?

Yes — 4.5 EUR. Madeira charges for several of its marked trails, and the fee is collected at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 2 by bus?

Partly. Line 81 reach one end only; the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift.

How high does PR 2 go?

It runs between 458 m and 1438 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing.

Is PR 2 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
