PR 2
Vereda do Urzalopen

Vereda do Urzal (PR 2) is a demanding 10.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Curral das Freiras to Lombo do Urzal. It climbs about 1269 m and descends about 1031 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 4.5 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
