PR 4
Levada do Barreiropartially open

Levada do Barreiro (PR 4) is a easy 5.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Poço da Neve to Casa do Barreiro. It climbs about 30 m and descends about 706 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 6 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (706 m down against 30 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 966 m and 1642 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 2 h 10. Access status is currently partially open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.

Common questions

How long does PR 4 Levada do Barreiro take?

The official walking time for PR 4 is 2 h 10 over 5.2 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 4?

No. This trail is free to walk; some other marked trails on Madeira are not.

Can I get to PR 4 by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 4 go?

It runs between 966 m and 1642 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing.

Is PR 4 open?

The last published status is partially open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
