Caminho do Norte (PR 21) is a easy 3.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Encumeada to Ribeira Grande. It climbs about 0 m and descends about 673 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
