PR 17
Caminho do Pináculo e Folhadalpartially open

Caminho do Pináculo e Folhadal (PR 17) is a demanding 15.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Lombo do Mouro to Encumeada. It climbs about 1258 m and descends about 940 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently partially open. An entry fee of 4.5 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
