PR 1.3
Vereda da Encumeadaclosed

Vereda da Encumeada (PR 1.3) is a demanding 11.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Casa de Abrigo do Pico Ruivo to Encumeada. It climbs about 1225 m and descends about 468 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
