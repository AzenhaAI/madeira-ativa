PR 1.3
Vereda da Encumeadaopen

Vereda da Encumeada (PR 1.3) is a moderate 11.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Casa de Abrigo do Pico Ruivo to Encumeada. It climbs about 447 m and descends about 1205 m along the way. That averages a gentle but steady gradient, about 40 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (1205 m down against 447 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 987 m and 1782 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 5 h. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Only one end is served by a bus (6); the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.
