PR 2 VE
Vereda do Pico do Castelo (Este)open

Vereda do Pico do Castelo (Este) (PR 2 VE) is a easy 4.6 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Moledo to Miradouro do Canhão. It climbs about 104 m and descends about 209 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
