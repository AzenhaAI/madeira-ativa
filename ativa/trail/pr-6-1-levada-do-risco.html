PR 6.1
Levada do Riscoopen

Levada do Risco (PR 6.1) is a easy 3.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Rabaçal (ER 105) to Risco. It climbs about 135 m and descends about 270 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 3 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
