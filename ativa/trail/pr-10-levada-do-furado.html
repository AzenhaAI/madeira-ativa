PR 10
Levada do Furadoopen

Levada do Furado (PR 10) is a moderate 11.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeiro Frio to Portela. It climbs about 16 m and descends about 251 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 1 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 638 m and 888 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 5 h. Access status is currently open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

On the path

  • No railing recorded on any section
  • 6 tunnels (100 m) — bring a torch

There are 6 tunnels on the route, 100 m in total. They are unlit: a torch or a head lamp is not optional, and a phone light is the usual substitute.

How to get there

Both ends are on a bus route (103, 138, 53, 56), so the walk can be done without a car. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.

Common questions

How long does PR 10 Levada do Furado take?

The official walking time for PR 10 is 5 h over 11.0 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 10?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 10 by bus?

Yes — lines 103, 138, 53, 56 serve the trail. Timetables are linked above.

How high does PR 10 go?

It runs between 638 m and 888 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level.

Are there tunnels on PR 10?

Yes — 6 of them, 100 m in total, and they are unlit. Bring a torch.

Is PR 10 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
