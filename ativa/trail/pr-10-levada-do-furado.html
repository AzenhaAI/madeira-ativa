PR 10
Levada do Furadoopen

Levada do Furado (PR 10) is a demanding 11.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeiro Frio to Portela. It climbs about 679 m and descends about 793 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
