PR 1
Vereda do Areeiropartially open

Vereda do Areeiro (PR 1) is a moderate 6.1 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Pico do Arieiro to Pico Ruivo. It climbs about 499 m and descends about 461 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 3 h. Access status is currently partially open. An entry fee of 10.50 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
