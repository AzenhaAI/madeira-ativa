PR 28
Levada da Rocha Vermelhaclosed

Levada da Rocha Vermelha (PR 28) is a moderate 10.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Fanal (ER 209) to Lombo do Atouguia. It climbs about 486 m and descends about 760 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
