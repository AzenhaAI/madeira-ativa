PR 13
Vereda do Fanalopen

Vereda do Fanal (PR 13) is a moderate 10.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Assobiadores to Fanal. It climbs about 235 m and descends about 539 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
