PR 13
Vereda do Fanalopen

Vereda do Fanal (PR 13) is a moderate 10.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Assobiadores to Fanal. It climbs about 235 m and descends about 539 m along the way. That averages a gentle but steady gradient, about 22 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (539 m down against 235 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 1129 m and 1434 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 4 h. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.
