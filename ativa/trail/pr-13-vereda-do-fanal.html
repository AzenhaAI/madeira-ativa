PR 13
Vereda do Fanalopen

Vereda do Fanal (PR 13) is a moderate 10.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Assobiadores to Fanal. It climbs about 59 m and descends about 323 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 5 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 1132 m and 1416 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 4 h. Access status is currently open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.

Common questions

How long does PR 13 Vereda do Fanal take?

The official walking time for PR 13 is 4 h over 10.8 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 13?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 13 by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 13 go?

It runs between 1132 m and 1416 m above sea level. At that height the trail is often above or inside the cloud, and noticeably colder than the coast, whatever Funchal is doing.

Is PR 13 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
