Glaciar de Planaltoclosed

Glaciar de Planalto (PR 27) is a moderate 10.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Bica da Cana to ER 105. It climbs about 282 m and descends about 263 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
