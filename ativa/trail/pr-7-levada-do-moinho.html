PR 7
Levada do Moinhoclosed

Levada do Moinho (PR 7) is a moderate 10.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeira da Cruz (ER 101) to Junqueira. It climbs about 510 m and descends about 0 m along the way. That averages a gentle but steady gradient, about 50 m of climb per kilometre. The route gains far more height than it loses (510 m up against 0 m down), so it is a climb for most of its length. It runs between 409 m and 930 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 3 h 30. Access status is currently closed. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.

Common questions

How long does PR 7 Levada do Moinho take?

The official walking time for PR 7 is 3 h 30 over 10.3 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 7?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 7 by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 7 go?

It runs between 409 m and 930 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level.

Is PR 7 open?

The last published status is closed. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
