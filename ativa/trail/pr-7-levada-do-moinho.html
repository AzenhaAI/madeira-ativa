PR 7
Levada do Moinhoclosed

Levada do Moinho (PR 7) is a demanding 10.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeira da Cruz (ER 101) to Junqueira. It climbs about 700 m and descends about 285 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
