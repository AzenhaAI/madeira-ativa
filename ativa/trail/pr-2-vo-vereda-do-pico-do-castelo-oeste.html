PR 2 VO
Vereda do Pico do Castelo (Oeste)open

Vereda do Pico do Castelo (Oeste) (PR 2 VO) is a easy 3.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Moledo to Miradouro do Canhão. It climbs about 82 m and descends about 167 m along the way. That averages a gentle but steady gradient, about 26 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 230 m and 373 m above sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 2 h 15. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. No car park is mapped within 600 m of either end.

Common questions

How long does PR 2 VO Vereda do Pico do Castelo (Oeste) take?

The official walking time for PR 2 VO is 2 h 15 over 3.2 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 2 VO?

No. This trail is free to walk; some other marked trails on Madeira are not.

Can I get to PR 2 VO by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 2 VO go?

It runs between 230 m and 373 m above sea level.

Is PR 2 VO open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
