PR 15
Vereda da Ribeira da Janelaopen

Vereda da Ribeira da Janela (PR 15) is a moderate 2.7 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Curral Falso to Ribeira da Janela. It climbs about 0 m and descends about 404 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 0 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (404 m down against 0 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 441 m and 845 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 1 h 30. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.
