PR 6
Levada das 25 Fontesopen

Levada das 25 Fontes (PR 6) is a easy 4.3 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Rabaçal (ER 105) to 25 Fontes. It climbs about 147 m and descends about 393 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
