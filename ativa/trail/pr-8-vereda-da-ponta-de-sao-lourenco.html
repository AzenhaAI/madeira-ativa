PR 8
Vereda da Ponta de São Lourençoopen

Vereda da Ponta de São Lourenço (PR 8) is a moderate 3.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Baia D'Abra to Casa do Sardinha. It climbs about 237 m and descends about 221 m along the way. That averages a sustained climb, about 79 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 0 m and 85 m above sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 2 h 30. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 3.00 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Only one end is served by a bus (113); the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.

Common questions

How long does PR 8 Vereda da Ponta de São Lourenço take?

The official walking time for PR 8 is 2 h 30 over 3.0 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 8?

Yes — 3.00 EUR. Madeira charges for several of its marked trails, and the fee is collected at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 8 by bus?

Partly. Line 113 reach one end only; the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift.

How high does PR 8 go?

It runs between 0 m and 85 m above sea level.

Is PR 8 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
