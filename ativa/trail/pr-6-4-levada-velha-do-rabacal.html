PR 6.4
Levada Velha do Rabaçalopen

Levada Velha do Rabaçal (PR 6.4) is a moderate 5.0 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Casa do Rabaçal to Pico Gordo (ER105). It climbs about 394 m and descends about 261 m along the way. That averages a sustained climb, about 79 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 1012 m and 1174 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.
