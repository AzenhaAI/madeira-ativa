PR 5
Vereda das Fundurasopen

Vereda das Funduras (PR 5) is a moderate 8.7 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Portela to Maroços. It climbs about 277 m and descends about 682 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
