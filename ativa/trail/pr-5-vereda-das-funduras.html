PR 5
Vereda das Fundurasopen

Vereda das Funduras (PR 5) is a moderate 8.7 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Portela to Maroços. It climbs about 18 m and descends about 381 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 2 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (381 m down against 18 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 203 m and 604 m above sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 3 h. Access status is currently open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Only one end is served by a bus (53); the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.

Common questions

How long does PR 5 Vereda das Funduras take?

The official walking time for PR 5 is 3 h over 8.7 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 5?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 5 by bus?

Partly. Line 53 reach one end only; the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift.

How high does PR 5 go?

It runs between 203 m and 604 m above sea level.

Is PR 5 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
