PR 6.3
Vereda da Lagoa do Ventoopen

Vereda da Lagoa do Vento (PR 6.3) is a easy 1.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Rabaçal to Lagoa do Vento. It climbs about 267 m and descends about 74 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. There is no entry fee. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
