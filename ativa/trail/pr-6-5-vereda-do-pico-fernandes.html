PR 6.5
Vereda do Pico Fernandesopen

Vereda do Pico Fernandes (PR 6.5) is a easy 2.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Paul da Serra to 25 Fontes. It climbs about 431 m and descends about 20 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
