PR 1.2
Vereda do Pico Ruivoopen

Vereda do Pico Ruivo (PR 1.2) is a moderate 2.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Achada do Teixeira to Pico Ruivo. It climbs about 292 m and descends about 38 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 1 h 30. Access status is currently open. An entry fee of 3 EUR applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
