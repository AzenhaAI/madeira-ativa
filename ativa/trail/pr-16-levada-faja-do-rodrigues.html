PR 16
Levada Fajã do Rodriguesopen

Levada Fajã do Rodrigues (PR 16) is a moderate 3.9 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ginjas to Ribeira do u. It climbs about 465 m and descends about 537 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
