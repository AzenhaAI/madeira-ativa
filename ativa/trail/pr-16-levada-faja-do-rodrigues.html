PR 16
Levada Fajã do Rodriguesclosed

Levada Fajã do Rodrigues (PR 16) is a moderate 3.9 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ginjas to Ribeira do Inferno. It climbs about 338 m and descends about 259 m along the way. That averages a sustained climb, about 87 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 608 m and 910 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 3 h 30. Access status is currently closed. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. A car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. A car park is mapped at one end only.

Common questions

How long does PR 16 Levada Fajã do Rodrigues take?

The official walking time for PR 16 is 3 h 30 over 3.9 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 16?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 16 by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 16 go?

It runs between 608 m and 910 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level.

Are there tunnels on PR 16?

Yes — 4 of them, 1253 m in total, and they are unlit. Bring a torch.

Is PR 16 open?

The last published status is closed. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
