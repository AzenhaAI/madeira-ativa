PR 3
Levada do Pico do Casteloclosed

Levada do Pico do Castelo (PR 3) is a moderate 4.1 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Camacha to Capela da Graça. It climbs about 35 m and descends about 70 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 9 m of climb per kilometre. It runs between 104 m and 178 m above sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 1 h 30. Access status is currently closed. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

No bus route is mapped near either end, so this one effectively needs a car or a transfer. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus. No car park is mapped within 600 m of either end.

Common questions

How long does PR 3 Levada do Pico do Castelo take?

The official walking time for PR 3 is 1 h 30 over 4.1 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 3?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 3 by bus?

Not directly — no bus route is mapped near either end.

How high does PR 3 go?

It runs between 104 m and 178 m above sea level.

Is PR 3 open?

The last published status is closed. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
