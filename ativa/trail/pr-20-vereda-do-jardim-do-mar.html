PR 20
Vereda do Jardim do Marclosed

Vereda do Jardim do Mar (PR 20) is a easy 1.9 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Prazeres to Jardim do Mar. It climbs about 412 m and descends about 935 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
