PR 19
Caminho Real do Paúl do Maropen

Caminho Real do Paúl do Mar (PR 19) is a moderate 1.8 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Prazeres to Paúl do Mar. It climbs about 0 m and descends about 464 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 0 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (464 m down against 0 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 66 m and 530 m above sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 1 h 20. Access status is currently open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Both ends are on a bus route (80), so the walk can be done without a car. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.

Common questions

How long does PR 19 Caminho Real do Paúl do Mar take?

The official walking time for PR 19 is 1 h 20 over 1.8 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 19?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 19 by bus?

Yes — line 80 serves the trail. Timetables are linked above.

How high does PR 19 go?

It runs between 66 m and 530 m above sea level.

Is PR 19 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
