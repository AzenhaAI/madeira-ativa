PR 14
Levada dos Cedrosclosed

Levada dos Cedros (PR 14) is a moderate 7.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Fanal to Curral Falso. It climbs about 236 m and descends about 526 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently closed. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
