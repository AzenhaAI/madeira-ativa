PR 3.1
Caminho Real do Montepartially open

Caminho Real do Monte (PR 3.1) is a easy 4.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeira das Cales to Monte. It climbs about 95 m and descends about 664 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently partially open. There is no entry fee. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
