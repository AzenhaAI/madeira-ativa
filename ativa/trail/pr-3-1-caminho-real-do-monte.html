PR 3.1
Caminho Real do Monteopen

Caminho Real do Monte (PR 3.1) is a moderate 4.2 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Ribeira das Cales to Monte. It climbs about 6 m and descends about 578 m along the way. That is close to level walking for most of the way, about 1 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (578 m down against 6 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 580 m and 1152 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 2 h. Access status is currently open. There is no entry fee. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Both ends are on a bus route (103, 138, 20, 21, 22, 48, 56), so the walk can be done without a car. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.

Common questions

How long does PR 3.1 Caminho Real do Monte take?

The official walking time for PR 3.1 is 2 h over 4.2 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 3.1?

No. This trail is free to walk; some other marked trails on Madeira are not.

Can I get to PR 3.1 by bus?

Yes — lines 103, 138, 20, 21, 22, 48, 56 serve the trail. Timetables are linked above.

How high does PR 3.1 go?

It runs between 580 m and 1152 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level.

Is PR 3.1 open?

The last published status is open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
