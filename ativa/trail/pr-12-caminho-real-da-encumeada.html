PR 12
Caminho Real da Encumeadapartially open

Caminho Real da Encumeada (PR 12) is a moderate 12.5 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Miradouro da Boca da Corrida to Encumeada. It climbs about 306 m and descends about 658 m along the way. That averages a gentle but steady gradient, about 24 m of climb per kilometre. The route loses far more height than it gains (658 m down against 306 m up), so walked in this direction it is mostly descent — hard on the knees rather than the lungs. It runs between 838 m and 1312 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level. It is a point-to-point route. The official walking time is 5 h. Access status is currently partially open. An entry fee applies. The trail is waymarked in yellow.

How to get there

Only one end is served by a bus (6); the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift. It is point-to-point, not a loop, so a car left at the start is a car you have to walk back to — most people arrange a drop-off, a taxi, or the bus.

Common questions

How long does PR 12 Caminho Real da Encumeada take?

The official walking time for PR 12 is 5 h over 12.5 km. That is the signed estimate at the trailhead, walking steadily and without long stops.

Is there a fee for PR 12?

Yes — an entry fee applies; the amount is not published with the route. It is paid at the trailhead or online in advance.

Can I get to PR 12 by bus?

Partly. Line 6 reach one end only; the other needs a car, a taxi or a lift.

How high does PR 12 go?

It runs between 838 m and 1312 m above sea level. Mid-altitude on Madeira means cloud is common even on a clear day at sea level.

Is PR 12 open?

The last published status is partially open. Closures on Madeira follow storms and landslides and change without much warning, so check the official status on the day.
