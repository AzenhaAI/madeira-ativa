PR 12
Caminho Real da Encumeadapartially open

Caminho Real da Encumeada (PR 12) is a demanding 12.5 km walking trail on Madeira, running from Miradouro da Boca da Corrida to Encumeada. It climbs about 1045 m and descends about 1332 m along the way. It is a point-to-point route. Access status is currently partially open. The trail is waymarked in yellow.
