Which Madeira trails can I reach by bus?

15 of the 44 marked trails have a bus route mapped at at least one end, and 5 of those are served at both ends — the ones you can walk end to end without a car. Lines are run by Horários do Funchal, Rodoeste and SAM depending on the side of the island; each trail page links the exact SIGA timetable for its stop. A trail served at one end only is still walkable by bus if you are happy to come back the same way.
