Which Madeira trails are short and easy?

21 of the 44 marked trails are 6 km or less with under 300 m of climb — short enough for an afternoon, and the sensible place to start if you are new to the island's paths or walking with children. Walking times below are the official signed estimates, which assume a steady pace and no long stops.
