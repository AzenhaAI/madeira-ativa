Madeira Ativa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Madeira Ativa is a free app that shows sport and cultural events, levada trails and heritage on the island of Madeira. We designed it to work without accounts and without collecting personal data.

Data we collect

Nothing about you. The app does not ask for a name, email, phone number or login. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers. The one thing that leaves your device is a question you choose to type into Ask AI — see below.

Ask AI

When you ask a question, the question and the public facts the app has put together for it (trail status, weather, buses and the like) are sent to our server at azenha.ai, which passes them to Anthropic (Claude) — or, if that is unavailable, to Cloudflare Workers AI — only to write the answer. We do not store your question, and it is not linked to you or your device: there is no account, and no identifier is sent with it. The AI provider processes it under its own commercial terms. Do not type personal information into a question.

Data stored on your device

  • Your favorites, reminders and theme/language preference are saved locally on your device only.
  • Event data is cached locally so the app works offline.

This information never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network

The app downloads public event, news and trail data from azenha.ai/ativa. These are simple file requests and include no personal information. Weather, sea and warning data come from Open-Meteo and IPMA and are requested with a trail's public coordinates, never yours. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap. Opening an event, trail or news link launches your browser and is subject to that site's own policy.

Notifications

If you enable a reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device for the day before the event. No reminder data is sent to any server.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Email agent@azenha.ai.

Madeira Ativa — Política de Privacidade

Dados recolhidos: nenhum sobre si. A app não pede conta, email nem início de sessão, e não usa analítica, publicidade ou rastreadores.

Ask AI: a pergunta que escrever, com os factos públicos reunidos para ela (estado dos percursos, tempo, autocarros), é enviada para o nosso servidor em azenha.ai e daí para a Anthropic (Claude) — ou, se indisponível, para o Cloudflare Workers AI — apenas para escrever a resposta. Não guardamos a pergunta e ela não fica ligada a si nem ao dispositivo. Não escreva dados pessoais numa pergunta.

Dados no dispositivo: favoritos, lembretes e preferências de tema/idioma são guardados apenas no seu dispositivo; os dados de eventos são guardados em cache para funcionar offline. Nada sai do telemóvel e tudo é removido ao desinstalar.

Rede: a app descarrega dados públicos de azenha.ai/ativa (sem informação pessoal); os mapas são da OpenStreetMap.

Notificações: os lembretes são locais, na véspera do evento; nada é enviado para servidores.

Contacto: agent@azenha.ai.
